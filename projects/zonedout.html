ZonedOut.

An archived, read-only standalone client-side Fabric mod targeting Minecraft 26.3. Preserved on GitLab for reference, its source tree features a Windows 8.1 Metro-inspired single-window UI, modular combat and utility features, stealth-oriented QoL tools, and a 36-module seed-analysis and world-generation suite.

International Duck/Migration
September 2026

Why we moved
to GitLab.

International Duck Incorporated moved its organization and active development to GitLab after multiple GitHub account actions disrupted the team. This page records what happened and where the project stands now.

CURRENT STATUS · SEPTEMBER 25, 2026GitLab is the primary home

0xFF remains an active International Duck member. His GitHub profile is unavailable following a ban, while his public work continues on GitLab.

  4. Sep 24, 2026 · 14:2004

    GitLab’s public record timestamps the group at 14:20 CEST. Venomous, the website, NullTrace, and ChampionsCode followed that afternoon, and the website was updated to treat GitLab as the primary source.

  5. Sep 25, 2026 · 20:0205

    0xFF created Beacon in the GitLab group, pushed its main branch, and updated its public documentation. The new Minecraft 26.2 utility mod became the group’s fifth public repository.

    Public GitLab record
The decision

Continuity over uncertainty.

The move was made so development, releases, issue tracking, and collaboration could continue while the appeals run. GitLab now serves as the primary home for the organization and actively maintained repositories.

What remains on GitHub

A visible legacy and backup.

The GitHub organization and its four earlier repositories are publicly visible again, but active development has moved. GitLab now holds all five current public repositories, including Beacon, and is the source linked throughout this site.

Primary home

Development continues on GitLab.

The migration keeps public source, releases, issues, and project history accessible while team members continue their work, regardless of individual GitHub account status.

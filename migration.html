International Duck/Migration
September 2026

Why we moved
to GitLab.

International Duck Incorporated moved its organization and active development to GitLab after multiple GitHub account actions disrupted the team. This page records what happened and where the project stands now.

CURRENT STATUSAppeals remain open

As of September 24, 2026, the reported account and organization appeals are still in progress.

Timeline

  3. Sep 24, 2026 · 09:43

The decision

Continuity over uncertainty.

The move was made so development, releases, issue tracking, and collaboration could continue while the appeals run. GitLab now serves as the primary home for the organization and actively maintained repositories.

What remains on GitHub

Original links stay intact.

Member profiles continue to link to their original GitHub accounts where available. Projects that still live only on GitHub—such as NullTrace and ChampionsCode—continue to point to their actual repositories.

New primary home

Development continues on GitLab.

The migration keeps public source, releases, and project history accessible while the outstanding appeals are reviewed.

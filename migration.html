International Duck/Migration
September–October 2026

Why we moved
to GitLab.

International Duck Incorporated moved active development to GitLab after access disruptions affected its GitHub organization and member accounts. On October 29, GitHub Support confirmed that restrictions had been removed from @3spress0. The team also reports that the IDI organization has been restored. GitLab remains active, and the dated timeline below preserves what was known during the migration. The support email was supplied by the account holder; the organization restoration is team-reported.

CURRENT STATUS · OCTOBER 29, 2026GitHub restrictions removed for @3spress0; IDI says its organization is restored

GitHub Support emailed @3spress0 on October 29, 2026 at 01:07 UTC: “After manually reviewing your account, we've removed the restrictions and things should be back to normal the next time you sign in.” The account holder supplied the email. IDI also reports that its organization has been unbanned/restored; that organization status is team-reported. The earlier appeal history below is preserved as a dated record, not a statement of current restriction.

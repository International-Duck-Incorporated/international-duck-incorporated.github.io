NullTrace.

NullTrace is a client-side Minecraft privacy layer designed to reduce unwanted fingerprinting and tracking. It brings identity protection, connection auditing, resource-pack safeguards, and user-visible risk information into one focused project.

Scope

Project direction

  • Client-brand and network-channel protection
  • Translation and known-pack probe defenses
  • Resource-pack sandboxing and local-address protection
  • Per-server policy and connection risk model
Development

Current position

The roadmap targets a transparent policy engine with Allow, Warn, Block, and Log outcomes. Planned audit tools explain each protection decision and surface remaining exposure to the user.
